Features

Everything a client should do,
without the bloat.

Add in seconds

Paste a magnet link or upload a .torrent file. Metadata arrives from peers; the real name shows up automatically.

Live progress

Percent, speed, ETA, seed and peer counts — all streaming in real time with smart auto-refresh that never overlaps.

Multiple torrents

Pause, resume, re-announce and remove any transfer without disturbing the rest of the swarm.

Direct file access

Per-file progress and one-click downloads of individual files — no need to wait for the whole torrent.

Private by default

Runs on your own machine or server. Your IP, your history, your files. Optional RC4 encryption for throttling ISPs.

Works everywhere

A responsive web UI for phones, tablets and desktops. Host the frontend anywhere, point it at your backend.

How it works

Three steps to a finished file.

01 — Add

Drop in a link

Paste a magnet or upload a .torrent file from the dashboard. Multiple transfers queue cleanly.

02 — Download

The engine negotiates

libtorrent talks to trackers, DHT and PeX to find peers and fetch pieces — tuned for throughput.

03 — Access

Grab what you need

Download complete files, wait for a specific one, or stream before the torrent finishes.

Speed

Tuned for throughput.

What the engine does for you

Peer discovery and disk I/O are configured aggressively so your connection — not the client — decides the ceiling.

  • Announces to every tracker and tier — not just one per tier.
  • DHT state persisted across restarts for instant peer discovery.
  • UpnP / NAT-PMP auto port-forwarding, no manual router config.
  • Configurable upload cap to keep your ACK channel open.
  • Peer inspector shows seed count and choke state per peer.
